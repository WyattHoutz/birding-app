Bird icon crop annotator

Drag a square around the bird you want kept — head to tail. The drag is locked to a square, because the icon is square and any other shape describes a crop that cannot exist. The tool reports two things: the slide position today’s cropper can actually use (one number, 0–1), and the full box, which only helps if we let the cropper zoom or pad. If your box is not the same size as the sliding square, it says so — that is the evidence for whether a square can hold the bird at all.
