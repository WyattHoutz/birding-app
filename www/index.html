                          🥚 Easy misses

                          Common birds in your counties that aren’t on your year list yet — ranked by how many different places reported them (≥40% of the last 30 sampled days). First run samples a month of checklists in the background; each day is cached, so later runs are instant.

                          🔥 Hot hotspots

                          Ranks nearby hotspots running hot — fresh sightings surging right now. Needs your Home location.

                          🥶 Cold hotspots

                          Overlooked gems — high all-time diversity hotspots that are currently quiet. Needs your Home location.

                          👥 Birder convoys

                          Detects birding groups (2+ people sharing 2+ hotspots in a day) from recent checklists — their route is a tested itinerary worth copying.

                          🛬 Migration outlook

                          Predicts which species should arrive or depart in the next 2 weeks, from 2 years of weekly checklist history. One-time build (resumable).
